Delete your Kwetu account

You can delete your Kwetu account and its personal details at any time. Kwetu is a restaurant finder and food ordering app for Tanzania.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Kwetu and sign in.
  2. Tap the Settings button (gear icon) at the top of the home screen.
  3. Tap Profile.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: without the app

Send us an email from the address you registered with, or tell us the phone number on your account, and ask us to delete your account. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Email: the Kwetu support email will be added here soon

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, phone number, email address and password.
  • Your name, phone number, email and delivery address on your past orders.
  • Your phone’s notification token.
  • Your name on any reviews you wrote.
  • Two-step sign-in (authenticator) settings and admin access, if you had them.
  • The details the app saved on your phone (cleared when you delete in the app, or when you uninstall it).

What is kept

  • Past orders, without your personal details, so restaurants keep a record of what they served.
  • The star rating and comment of reviews you wrote, shown as “Former customer”.
  • If you own a restaurant: the restaurant is hidden from the app and no longer linked to you. Its past orders stay as part of those records.

Kept records no longer contain your name, phone number, email or address.

Futa akaunti yako ya Kwetu

Unaweza kufuta akaunti yako ya Kwetu na taarifa zake binafsi wakati wowote. Kwetu ni programu ya kutafuta migahawa na kuagiza chakula Tanzania.

Njia ya 1: ndani ya programu (haraka zaidi)

  1. Fungua Kwetu na uingie kwenye akaunti yako.
  2. Gusa kitufe cha Mipangilio (alama ya gia) juu ya skrini ya mwanzo.
  3. Gusa Wasifu.
  4. Gusa Futa akaunti na uthibitishe.

Akaunti inafutwa papo hapo.

Njia ya 2: bila programu

Tutumie barua pepe kutoka kwenye anwani uliyojisajilia nayo, au utuambie namba ya simu ya akaunti yako, na uombe tufute akaunti yako. Tunaifuta ndani ya siku 30 na tunakujibu ikikamilika.

Barua pepe: barua pepe ya msaada ya Kwetu itaongezwa hapa hivi karibuni

Kinachofutwa

  • Akaunti yako: jina, namba ya simu, barua pepe na nenosiri.
  • Jina lako, namba ya simu, barua pepe na anwani ya kuletewa kwenye oda zako za zamani.
  • Alama ya taarifa (notification token) ya simu yako.
  • Jina lako kwenye maoni uliyoandika.
  • Mipangilio ya kuingia kwa hatua mbili (authenticator) na ruhusa ya msimamizi, kama ulikuwa nazo.
  • Maelezo ambayo programu ilihifadhi kwenye simu yako (yanafutwa ukifuta ndani ya programu, au ukiondoa programu).

Kinachobaki

  • Oda za zamani, bila taarifa zako binafsi, ili migahawa iwe na kumbukumbu ya walichohudumia.
  • Nyota na maoni ya ukaguzi uliouandika, yakionyeshwa kama “Former customer” (mteja wa zamani).
  • Kama unamiliki mgahawa: mgahawa unafichwa kwenye programu na hauhusishwi nawe tena. Oda zake za zamani zinabaki kama sehemu ya kumbukumbu hizo.

Kumbukumbu zinazobaki hazina tena jina lako, namba ya simu, barua pepe wala anwani.
