Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Kwetu helps you find restaurants and order food. This page explains what information the app uses and how you stay in control of it.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and, if you give it, your email address, so restaurants can confirm your order.
  • Your delivery address for delivery orders, and the dishes, totals and times of your orders.
  • Account details if you register: your full name, phone number, whether you are a customer or a restaurant owner and, if you sign up with email, your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our hosting provider).
  • A notification token for your phone if you allow notifications, so we can tell you when your order is ready.
  • For restaurant owners: restaurant details, menus, the photos and menu PDF you upload, and the restaurant’s map location if you add it.
  • Ratings and comments you choose to leave about restaurants after a completed order.

What we do not collect

  • If you turn on location, the app uses your phone’s position only on your phone, to show how far restaurants are. It is never sent to Kwetu or stored, and you can turn it off at any time in your phone settings.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Kwetu takes no payments. You pay the restaurant in cash, so we never collect card or mobile money details.

Who can see it

  • The restaurant you order from sees your name, phone number, email, delivery address and order.
  • If you review a restaurant, your star rating, comment and first name are shown publicly on its page. Deleting your account removes your name from your reviews.
  • Kwetu administrators can see restaurant listings to review them, and can look up orders (including the customer’s name and phone number) to help customers and restaurants with problems.
  • Data is stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) and notifications are delivered through Expo. Sign-in codes are sent by a Tanzanian SMS company, which receives only your phone number and the code. They process it only to run the app.

How long we keep it

  • Orders are kept so restaurants have a record of what they served.
  • Details you save on your phone stay there until you delete them or uninstall the app.

Deleting your data

  • Signed-in users can delete their account at any time from Settings → Profile → Delete account. This removes the account, removes your name, phone number and address from past orders, hides any restaurant you own, and clears the data saved on your phone.
  • If you use the app without an account, your details are stored only on your phone and are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Full steps, including how to ask without the app: Delete your Kwetu account.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: the Kwetu support email will be added here soon.

Sera ya faragha

Ilisasishwa mwisho 20 November 2026

Kwetu inakusaidia kupata migahawa na kuagiza chakula. Ukurasa huu unaeleza taarifa ambazo programu inatumia na jinsi unavyoendelea kuzidhibiti.

Tunachokusanya

  • Jina lako, namba yako ya simu na, ukiitoa, barua pepe yako, ili migahawa iweze kuthibitisha oda yako.
  • Anwani yako ya kuletewa kwa oda za kuletewa, pamoja na vyakula, jumla na saa za oda zako.
  • Taarifa za akaunti ukijisajili: jina lako kamili, namba ya simu, kama wewe ni mteja au mmiliki wa mgahawa na, ukijisajili kwa barua pepe, barua pepe yako na nenosiri (linalohifadhiwa na mtoa huduma wetu wa uhifadhi kwa njia salama iliyosimbwa tu).
  • Alama ya taarifa (notification token) ya simu yako ukiruhusu taarifa, ili tukujulishe oda yako ikiwa tayari.
  • Kwa wamiliki wa migahawa: maelezo ya mgahawa, menyu, picha na PDF ya menyu unazopakia, na eneo la mgahawa kwenye ramani ukiliongeza.
  • Ukadiriaji na maoni unayochagua kuacha kuhusu migahawa baada ya oda kukamilika.

Tusichokusanya

  • Ukiwasha eneo, programu inatumia mahali simu yako ilipo kwenye simu yako tu, kuonyesha umbali wa migahawa. Halitumwi kwa Kwetu wala kuhifadhiwa, na unaweza kulizima wakati wowote kwenye mipangilio ya simu yako.
  • Hatuuzi taarifa zako wala kuzitumia kwa matangazo.
  • Kwetu haipokei malipo. Unalipa mgahawa kwa taslimu, kwa hiyo hatukusanyi kamwe taarifa za kadi wala za pesa ya simu.

Nani anaweza kuziona

  • Mgahawa unaoagiza kwake unaona jina lako, namba ya simu, barua pepe, anwani ya kuletewa na oda yako.
  • Ukitoa maoni kuhusu mgahawa, ukadiriaji wako wa nyota, maoni na jina lako la kwanza vinaonekana hadharani kwenye ukurasa wake. Ukifuta akaunti yako, jina lako linaondolewa kwenye maoni yako.
  • Wasimamizi wa Kwetu wanaweza kuona matangazo ya migahawa ili kuyakagua, na wanaweza kutafuta oda (pamoja na jina na namba ya simu ya mteja) ili kusaidia wateja na migahawa wanapopata matatizo.
  • Taarifa zinahifadhiwa na mtoa huduma wetu wa uhifadhi (Supabase) na taarifa za simu zinatumwa kupitia Expo. Misimbo ya kuingia inatumwa na kampuni ya SMS ya Tanzania, inayopokea namba yako ya simu na msimbo tu. Wanazishughulikia kwa ajili ya kuendesha programu tu.

Tunazihifadhi kwa muda gani

  • Oda huhifadhiwa ili migahawa iwe na kumbukumbu ya walichohudumia.
  • Maelezo unayohifadhi kwenye simu yako yanabaki hapo hadi uyafute au uondoe programu.

Kufuta taarifa zako

  • Watumiaji walioingia wanaweza kufuta akaunti yao wakati wowote kupitia Mipangilio → Wasifu → Futa akaunti. Hii inaondoa akaunti, inaondoa jina lako, namba ya simu na anwani kwenye oda za zamani, inaficha mgahawa wowote unaoumiliki, na inafuta taarifa zilizohifadhiwa kwenye simu yako.
  • Ukitumia programu bila akaunti, maelezo yako yanahifadhiwa kwenye simu yako tu na yanaondolewa unapoondoa programu.
  • Hatua kamili, pamoja na jinsi ya kuomba bila programu: Futa akaunti yako ya Kwetu.
